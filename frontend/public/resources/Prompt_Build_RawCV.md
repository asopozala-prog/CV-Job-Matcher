# Help Me Build My CV Raw Materials

You are helping me prepare a complete and reusable source document for future CV applications.

I may give you:

* an old CV in PDF
* Word or text documents
* screenshots or images
* LinkedIn information
* certificates
* project descriptions
* portfolio links
* GitHub links
* additional career notes

If I already have an old CV, use it as the starting point. I do not want to manually rewrite my CV just to begin this process.

## Your Task

Read everything I provide and create one clean Markdown document containing my available professional information.

The goal is NOT to create a polished CV for one particular job.

The goal is to create accurate, reusable **raw CV materials** that another AI can later use to build targeted CVs for different job applications.

Preserve useful detail rather than compressing everything into a traditional one- or two-page CV.

## Organize the Material

Use the following structure where information is available:

# CV RAW MATERIALS

## Personal & Contact Information

* Full name
* City / Country
* Email
* Phone
* LinkedIn
* Portfolio / Website
* GitHub or other relevant professional links

## Professional Experience

For each position preserve:

### Job Title

**Company / Organization** | Location | Dates

* responsibilities
* projects
* achievements
* scope of work
* relevant tools or methods
* measurable results, if actually supported by my materials

Keep company, job title, location, and dates as separate facts.

Do not merge them.

## Education

For each entry:

* Degree / qualification
* Institution
* Location if available
* Dates / graduation year
* Relevant specialization if supported

## Certifications & Professional Training

For each entry:

* Certification / training
* Issuer
* Date
* Credential information if available

## Skills & Tools

Collect supported professional skills, methods, technologies, tools, platforms, and domain knowledge from my materials.

Do not limit this to the small number of skills normally shown on a finished CV.

This is a source library for later selection.

## Languages

For each language:

* Language
* Proficiency level exactly as supported by my information

## Projects / Builds

Preserve meaningful professional, technical, academic, freelance, or independent projects.

For each project, include where available:

* Project name
* What I built or did
* Problem or purpose
* My role
* Relevant methods / technologies
* Result or outcome
* Link

## Additional Relevant Professional Information

Include other useful information that does not fit naturally above.

---

## Professional Positioning Material

After organizing the factual information, create a short reusable section based ONLY on evidence in my materials.

### Professional Strengths

Identify the strongest recurring professional capabilities demonstrated by my background.

### Professional Title Options

Suggest several realistic professional titles that could describe my existing or emerging professional direction.

These are positioning suggestions, not claims of employment history.

Clearly keep them separate from my actual historical job titles.

### Skill Keyword Pool

Create a broader list of factual keywords that future CVs could select from.

Only include keywords supported by my experience, education, projects, or training.

### Summary Material

Write 2–4 short professional summary variants representing the strongest defensible ways my background could be positioned.

Do not target a specific vacancy yet.

---

# Accuracy Check

Before finalizing the Markdown, inspect my materials for possible problems.

Look for:

* missing dates
* unclear employment periods
* missing company or organization names
* missing locations
* unexplained career gaps
* overlapping dates that may need clarification
* inconsistent job titles
* conflicting dates
* inconsistent education information
* unclear certification status
* suspicious or contradictory proficiency levels
* broken or incomplete professional links
* achievements without enough supporting information
* important experience mentioned in one source but missing from another
* projects whose ownership or role is unclear

Do NOT silently correct uncertain information.

Do NOT guess.

Do NOT invent missing details.

If something appears contradictory, preserve the source information and ask me to clarify it.

## Fundamental CV Information Check

Also tell me if important CV information appears to be missing, especially:

* full name
* location
* professional contact information
* employment dates
* employer / organization names
* job titles
* education dates
* language levels
* relevant links where expected

Not every candidate needs every item, so only flag things that genuinely need attention.

---

# Interaction

If the supplied materials are sufficient:

1. Create the Markdown document.
2. Then give me a short section called **"Please Check"** containing only the uncertainties or missing information I should review.

If important information is unclear:

Do not stop the entire extraction.

First build everything that can be established safely from the materials, then list the questions that need my confirmation.

Keep the number of questions reasonable and prioritize issues that could materially affect a future CV.

If I later provide corrections or additional documents, update the Markdown rather than starting over.

---

# Important Rules

* Treat my supplied materials as the factual source of truth.
* Never invent career information.
* Preserve useful details even if they would not all appear in a finished CV.
* Distinguish factual career history from AI-generated positioning suggestions.
* Do not optimize for a specific job yet.
* Do not exaggerate seniority, expertise, achievements, or language ability.
* If something is uncertain, mark it for confirmation instead of guessing.

Output the main CV raw materials as clean Markdown that I can save as a `.md` file and reuse with other AI tools.
