KIRON WELCOME PAGE — Layout Plan
SECTION 1 — Hero / Welcome
Kiron prominent, standing or walking pose.
Large, calm. She speaks first.

"Hi, I'm Kiron. I help you show up correctly for the right opportunity."

No buttons yet. Just presence.
Background: soft, light. Her teal against white or near-white.

SECTION 2 — Why a CV exists
Kiron: thinking or curious expression — small, sidebar presence.
Content: 3 short lines. No list. Prose.
Ends with the reframe: "It's not about your value. It's about the right match."

SECTION 3 — The CV's only mission
Kiron: encouraging expression.
Three tasks displayed visually — not a wall of text.
Simple horizontal or vertical flow: Machine → HR → Responsible person
One line each. Arrow or step logic. Clean.

SECTION 4 — Why materials, not a CV
Kiron: supportive expression, holding heart.
Short paragraph. Ends with the handoff:
"You focus on what you've done. She handles the rest."

SECTION 5 — What to prepare
Three blocks — ① ② ③
Each block has a small Kiron icon from the UI set.
① analysis icon — essentials
② good match icon — who you are
③ probability / builds icon — your story

Short text per block. Kiron's voice stays warm.

SECTION 6 — Input form entry
Smooth transition. No hard break.
Kiron: focused expression, at laptop.
Three input areas appear:

Job offer + company info
Your professional identity (② material)
Your story (③ material)
Simple, spacious. One clear action button at the bottom.
"Let Kiron work" or similar.

Visual rhythm:
Each section: Kiron pose/expression shifts.
She's present but never overwhelming.
Content stays left or center. She anchors right or top depending on section.
