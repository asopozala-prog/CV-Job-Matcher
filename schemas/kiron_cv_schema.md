# Kiron CV Schema — Two-Page Structure

---

## PAGE 1 — HR / 10-Second Human Scan
*Single column, strict. No decorative elements.*

---

### HEADER
Full name — Email — Phone — LinkedIn URL — Location (City, Country)
Website or other relevant links if available.
*(No photo, no date of birth, no full address)*

---

### TITLE LINE
One line. Matches the job title from the offer exactly.
*Example: "Project Manager | AI & Automation Systems"*

---

### SUMMARY
2 sentences.
Who you are + what you bring → matched to the job requirement.

---

### EXPERIENCE
Reverse chronological.
Each role: **Job Title — Company — Dates**
2–4 bullet points per role.
Format: *Action verb + what you did + result/impact*
Keywords embedded naturally — pointing to working role and responsibility.

---

### SKILLS
5 chosen keywords or key terms — no more.
Hard skills first, tools, then soft skills if relevant.
*(ATS scores here)*

---

### HIGHLIGHT LINE
From either Projects or Education — choose the single most relevant one.
Display in one line only.
*Example: "Built KIRON — local-first AI assistant, live demo available"*
*or: "MSc Computer Science — TU Berlin, 2021"*

---
---

## PAGE 2 — Domain Expert / Depth
*For the person who actually understands the work.*

---

### PROJECTS / BUILDS
For each project:
**Project Name** — One-line description — Link
*What problem it solved.*

---

### EXPERIENCE (EXTENDED)
Additional detail on roles where relevant.
Context, scope, constraints — what page 1 condensed.

---

### EDUCATION
Degree — Institution — Year
Remove irrelevant ones. No descriptions unless recent graduate.

---

### CERTIFICATIONS
Name — Issuer — Year

---

### LANGUAGES
Language — Level
