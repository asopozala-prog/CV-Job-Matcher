# Before you send your information

Kiron is a free, non-commercial online app demo. It uses the Google Gemini Free Tier API to analyse the job offer and career information you submit.

Your submitted information is therefore sent to Google Gemini for AI processing. If you have privacy concerns, please review the specific terms and data-handling policies that apply to Google's Gemini API Free Tier before using Kiron. Kiron does not interpret or make guarantees about Google's policies.

Kiron itself uses your information only to produce your job-match evaluation and tailored CV. Processing files are kept temporarily and deleted after the job is completed and the results have been delivered.

Want a different setup? You can contact the developer to discuss a customized version using your own API key and deployment environment.
