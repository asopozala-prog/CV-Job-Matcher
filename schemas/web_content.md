**WHY A CV EXISTS**

When a company hires, it's a big investment.
Time, money, contracts, team integration.
They're not asking *"is this person good?"*
They're asking *"is this the right match for this role?"*

A CV is not your value. It's a matching tool.
Think of it like finding the right fit — in millions of possibilities.
Not every role is yours. And that's okay.

---

**THE CV'S ONLY MISSION**

One CV. One job offer. Three tasks:

→ Pass the machine — ATS scans for keywords and structure
→ Give HR clarity in 10 seconds — enough to forward it
→ Show the responsible person you could be the right one

After that — it's the interview.
The CV just opens the door.

---

**WHY MATERIALS, NOT A CV**

You don't need to worry about format, wording, or selection.
Just gather your information — once, clearly.

Think of it as your personal career dataset.
Kiron selects what's relevant for each offer.
You focus on what you've done. She handles the rest.

---

**WHAT TO PREPARE**

**① Must have** — without these, the CV is incomplete
- Full name, contact, location
- Current or most recent job title
- Work history with dates
